# Avatar bandwidth curve (phase 6)

Measured wire cost of the avatar pipeline per active avatar. Generated by
`node tests/bench-avatar.mjs` — fully deterministic (synthetic gesture
profiles, seeded PRNG), so the tables below reproduce exactly. Real-world
numbers under throttled networks land in phase 9 (`BANDWIDTH_REPORT.md`).

## Method

- Frame format: fixed 13 B binary frames (see `NETWORK_PROTOCOL.md` §8).
- Send policy (`public/js/avatar/optimize.js`): a pose is transmitted only if
  it moved beyond a deadband since the last **sent** pose; otherwise suppressed.
  A keepalive is force-sent every 400 ms (< 5 s receiver TTL) so quiet avatars
  stay alive.
- Deadbands are fractions of each axis range — shape axes are 0..1, angles ±80°:
  - `light`: shape 0.015 / angle 0.025
  - `default`: shape 0.03 / angle 0.05
  - `heavy`: shape 0.06 / angle 0.10
- Profiles (30 s sessions): `idle` (micro-noise + blinks), `talk`
  (~2.7 Hz jaw syllables + small head motion), `animated` (full head sweeps,
  brows, pucker, speech).

## Reading the curve

1. **Idle dominates real usage** — people mostly sit still while listening.
   Suppression collapses idle cost to the keepalive floor
   (2.5 keepalives/s × 13 B ≈ 33 B/s; measured 36–37 with blinks) — **~4×
   cheaper than raw 12 Hz**, and independent of Hz. Without it, raising Hz
   multiplies cost even for motionless users.
2. **Talking benefits moderately**: default deadband at 12 Hz costs 127 B/s
   (−19 %); `heavy` reaches 108 B/s (−31 %) at some perceptual risk (chopped
   small gestures).
3. **Animated motion barely compresses** via deadband alone (−3 %): genuine
   movement always exceeds thresholds. For that regime only lower Hz helps;
   delta/mask encoding was evaluated and rejected — with ≤14 B worst-case
   frames the mask header eats most of the win and adds protocol surface.
4. **Budget check @120 kbps (15 kB/s)**: one-way room of 8 avatars at
   defaults ≈ 8 × ~130 B/s ≈ 1 kB/s ≈ **7 % of uplink** — comfortable. Even
   the 20 Hz × none worst case (260 B/s each) would be ~17 % for 8 members.

## Shipped defaults & knobs

- Defaults: 12 Hz, deadband `default` (0.03/0.05).
- URL knobs for live experiments (phase 9 will use them):
  - `?avhz=8` — tracking/send rate, clamped [2, 30]
  - `?avdb=0.06` — deadband fraction applied to both axis groups (0 disables)
- Verify in-browser: stats card "Avatar traffic" row should match the table
  for the selected config within jitter.
